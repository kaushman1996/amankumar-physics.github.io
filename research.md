# Research

## Generalized Wigner Crystals in Moiré Systems

I developed the first finite‑temperature quantum theory of generalized Wigner crystals in triangular moiré heterostructures. Long‑range interactions and charge frustration stabilize unconventional crystalline and partially melted phases, including pinball‑type states, explaining discrepancies between experiment and zero‑temperature theory.

Key references:  
- *npj Quantum Materials* **10**, 95 (2025)  
- arXiv:2604.19870

---

## Quantum Thermal Melting

I studied how quantum effects modify thermal melting transitions. For first‑order transitions, I showed that quantum fluctuations can *increase* melting temperatures despite softening of order parameters, driven by entropy–energy competition.

---

## Thermal Hall Transport in Kitaev Materials

I developed tensor‑network‑based approaches to compute thermal Hall conductivity directly from edge currents, reproducing half‑quantized plateaus in the Kitaev limit and explaining large unquantized signals observed in α‑RuCl₃.

---

## Quasiparticle Stability

I reformulated quasiparticle stability as localization in many‑body Hilbert space, using FEAST exact diagonalization to study quasiparticle metamorphosis across different interaction regimes.
