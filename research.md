# Research

## Generalized Wigner Crystals in Moiré Systems

My research provides the first finite‑temperature quantum theory of generalized Wigner crystals in triangular moiré heterostructures. I showed that long‑range interactions and charge frustration stabilize unconventional crystalline and partially melted phases, including pinball‑type states, and explain experimentally observed asymmetries in melting temperatures.

Key references:  
- *npj Quantum Materials* **10**, 95 (2025)  
- arXiv:2604.19870

---

## Quantum Thermal Melting

I investigated how quantum effects modify thermal melting transitions in charge‑ordered systems. In contrast to classical expectations, I showed that quantum fluctuations can *increase* the melting temperature for first‑order transitions despite softening order parameters, a phenomenon explained by entropy–energy competition.

---

## Thermal Hall Transport in Kitaev Materials

I developed a tensor‑network‑based approach to compute thermal Hall conductivity directly from edge currents, without assuming quasiparticle descriptions. This method reproduces half‑quantized plateaus in the Kitaev limit and explains large unquantized thermal Hall signals observed experimentally in materials such as α‑RuCl₃.

Key reference:  
- *Phys. Rev. B* **107**, L220406 (2023)

---

## Quasiparticle Stability and Hilbert‑Space Localization

I reformulated quasiparticle stability as a localization phenomenon in the many‑body Hilbert space. Using FEAST exact diagonalization, I studied quasiparticle metamorphosis across parameter regimes, treating conventional and fractionalized excitations on equal footing.

Key reference:  
- *Phys. Rev. B* **106**, L081120 (2022)
