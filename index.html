About

I am a condensed matter theorist working on emergent phenomena in strongly correlated quantum systems, including electronic crystallinity, fractionalization, topological order, and transport. My research combines large‑scale exact diagonalization, finite‑temperature methods, and tensor‑network techniques to connect microscopic models with experimentally observable signatures.

Research Interests

  • Wigner crystals and charge order in triangular moiré systems
  • Pinball phases and metal–insulator transitions
  • Fractionalization and topological order in frustrated magnets
  • Thermal Hall transport in Kitaev materials
  • Quasiparticle stability in many‑body Hilbert space
