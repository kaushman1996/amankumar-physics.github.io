Research

My research focuses on emergent phenomena in strongly correlated quantum many‑body systems, where long‑range interactions, frustration, and quantum fluctuations produce unconventional electronic order and transport.

Generalized Wigner Crystals

Finite‑temperature quantum theory of Wigner crystals in triangular moiré systems, including pinball phases and anomalous melting behavior.

Thermal Hall Transport

Tensor‑network‑based calculations of thermal Hall conductivity in Kitaev materials such as α‑RuCl₃.

Quasiparticle Stability

Localization‑based understanding of quasiparticle stability in many‑body Hilbert space using large‑scale exact diagonalization.
